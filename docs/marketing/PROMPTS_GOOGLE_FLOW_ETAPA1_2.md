    - **Sujeto Principal & Acción**: Futuristic 3D typographic letterforms resembling speed lines shattering the sound barrier.

    - **Cámara & Iluminación**: Frontal close-up, dynamic wide angle distortion.
    - **Atmósfera & Color**: Pitch black #050810 atmosphere with bright neon blue #3b82f6 edge lighting.
